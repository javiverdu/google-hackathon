# Erasmus Office FAQ (demo data)

## When is the deadline to submit the Learning Agreement?
The signed Learning Agreement must be uploaded within 30 days of the start of the stay. For the 2026-27 year the office deadline is 15 October 2026.

## Who signs my Learning Agreement?
The student signs first, then the academic coordinator at the home university, then the host university coordinator. The Erasmus office only checks the final signed PDF.

## How do I know if you received my insurance?
The agent adds the document to your row in students.xlsx as soon as it is processed. If the Missing column still lists Insurance, it has not been received yet.

## Which insurance policy is accepted?
Any policy with at least 30,000 EUR medical coverage and full coverage for the whole stay. The certificate must show the start and end dates.

## Do I need a Certificate of Arrival?
Yes. It must be signed and stamped by the host university within the first two weeks after arrival.

## What documents are required for every student?
Learning Agreement, Transcript, Insurance and Certificate of Arrival.

## How long does document processing take?
Documents sent by email are processed automatically and normally appear in the spreadsheet the same working day.

## My file was rejected, what now?
If the scan is blurry or the student name cannot be read, the document is sent to the review folder and a staff member checks it manually. You will receive an email if a new copy is needed.

## Can I send documents as photos?
Yes, JPG and PNG photos are accepted if the text is readable and all four corners of the page are visible.
